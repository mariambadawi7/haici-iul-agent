# IUL Financial Information — Fees and Scholarships

## Application and Registration Fees
- Application file fees must be paid at the Accounting Department (Financial Affairs Unit) in advance
- These fees are non-refundable
- Students are not officially registered until course fees for the semester are paid

## Scholarships Available
IUL offers the following types of financial support:
- Merit-Based Scholarships: for top-ranking high school students
- Athletic Scholarships: for students with exceptional athletic achievements
- Financial Aid Plans: flexible plans for students demonstrating financial need

## Social Security (CNSS)
Students must provide a Social Security (CNSS) affiliation certificate as part of their admission documents. This is required for both undergraduate and graduate admissions.

## Tuition Refund Policy
Refund conditions and modalites are defined by the university's financial regulations. Students who withdraw from courses may be eligible for partial refunds depending on the timing of withdrawal relative to the academic calendar.

## Fees for Special Programs
- Pre-Doc (PhD Preliminary Semester): Registration fees apply for the Pré-Doc semester (Spring 2026). The exact amount is announced when the student submits a complete file to the Admissions and Orientation Office.
- PhD Program: 180 ECTS credits total. Fees are paid per semester. After paying for all 180 credits, only administrative registration fees apply for additional years.
- Summer Session: Available for students needing 1-16 credits to complete their degree. Starts third week of June, runs maximum 10 weeks.
