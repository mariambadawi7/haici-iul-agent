### Admissions and Financial Aid
* **Application Deadlines:** The deadline for the Fall Semester is August 30th. The deadline for the Spring Semester is January 15th.
* **Application Process:** Prospective students must submit a completed online application, official high school transcripts (Lebanese Baccalaureate or equivalent), a photocopy of their ID/Passport, and passport-sized photos.
* **Language Requirements:** Depending on the major, students may need to pass an English or French proficiency exam or enroll in the university's intensive language center.
* **Scholarships:** IUL offers Merit-Based Scholarships for top-ranking high school students, Athletic Scholarships, and flexible financial aid plans for students demonstrating financial need.

### Campus Locations and Navigation
* **Wardanieh Campus (Main Campus):** Located in the Chouf District. This campus houses the central administration, the main central library, the primary IT Helpdesk, and the grand cafeteria. It features modern engineering labs and the HAICI research center.
* **Khaldeh Campus:** Located on the Old Saida Road, south of Beirut. It is highly accessible for students commuting from the capital and features dedicated faculty buildings and a student lounge.
* **Tyre (Sour) Campus:** Serving South Lebanon, providing state-of-the-art classrooms and local administrative support.
* **Baalbek Campus:** Serving the Bekaa region with dedicated academic facilities and local registration offices.

### Academic Calendar
* **Fall Semester:** Begins in the second week of September and concludes with final exams in late January.
* **Spring Semester:** Begins in the second week of February and concludes with final exams in late June.
* **Summer Semester:** An optional intensive 6-week term running from July to mid-August.
