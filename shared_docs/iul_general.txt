# Islamic University of Lebanon (IUL) — General Information

## Overview
The Islamic University of Lebanon (IUL) is a private, non-profit higher education institution in Lebanon. It was officially established in 1996 by Lebanese Presidential Decree No. 8600. Its motto is "A University for all country citizens and the Arab world."

## History and Foundation
IUL was inspired by the founding vision of Imam Sayyed Moussa El-Sader, who aimed to make higher education accessible to all segments of Lebanese society, particularly the marginalized. The vision was brought to life by the late Imam Sheikh Mohammad Mahdi Shams Al-Din and later expanded by Imam Sheikh Abdul Amir Qablan.

IUL operates under the umbrella of the Supreme Islamic Shiite Council in Lebanon but maintains administrative, educational, and financial autonomy.

## Accreditation and Rankings
- All degrees are fully recognized by the Lebanese Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE).
- IUL holds a prestigious 5-year unreserved institutional accreditation from HCERES (the French High Council for Evaluation of Research and Higher Education), marking compliance with international and European educational standards.
- IUL is ranked in the #1001-1200 bracket globally in the QS World University Rankings and within the top 100 in the Arab Region Rankings.

## Languages of Instruction
Programs are taught in Arabic, English, and French depending on the specialization. Many programs in Engineering, Business, and Health Sciences are taught in English and French. The university has a dedicated Language Center.

## Student Life
- IUL hosts over 30 active student societies ranging from sports and arts to programming, debate, and translation clubs.
- Graduates from faculties like Engineering and Law are fully eligible to join Lebanese professional syndicates (e.g., the Order of Engineers and Architects, and the Bar Associations).

## Academic Centers
- Research Center (REC): Promotes research quality and public service across faculties.
- Center for Continuing Education (CCE): Lifelong learning programs, professional training, and language courses.
- Learning and Teaching Excellence Center (LTEC): Supports faculty in developing teaching methods and academic performance.
- Language Center (LCR): Serves students who need language support in English, French, or other languages. Collaborates with British Council, Institut Français, Goethe Institute, and others.
- Human Rights Center (HRC): Promotes human rights culture, tolerance, and civic responsibility.
- Career Center (CRC): Helps graduates with career planning, job fairs, and employment opportunities.
