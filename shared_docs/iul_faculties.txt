# IUL Faculties and Academic Programs

IUL offers Undergraduate (Bachelor's/License), Graduate (Master's), and Postgraduate (PhD) programs.

## Faculty of Engineering
Located primarily at the Wardanieh campus. IUL was the first university in Lebanon to offer specialized degrees in Biomedical Engineering and Surveying Engineering.

Bachelor's programs (5-year Engineering Diploma):
- Computer and Communications Engineering
- Surveying Engineering
- Biomedical Engineering Technologies
- Civil Engineering

Master's programs:
- Master's in Biomedical Engineering Technologies
- Master's in Computer Science and Communications

## Faculty of Law
A highly regarded faculty established by Decree No. 1948 dated 21/12/1999.

Bachelor's programs (License - 3 years, under Lebanese University supervision):
- Law (general)

Diploma of Higher Studies:
- Private Law
- Business Law
- Public Law

Master's programs:
- Private Law
- Business Law
- Public Law
- Arbitration (pending)
- Administrative Law (pending)

PhD programs:
- PhD in Law
