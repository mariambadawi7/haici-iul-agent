# HAICI — Humanity-AI Driven Center for Innovation

## Overview
The Humanity-AI Driven Center for Innovation (HAICI) is a premier research and development hub affiliated with the Islamic University of Lebanon (IUL). It is located at the Wardanieh campus.

## Mission and Focus
HAICI is dedicated to bridging advanced artificial intelligence technologies with human-centric solutions. The center focuses on:
- AI ethics
- Digital transformation
- Empowering youth in the digital sphere
- Developing innovative AI-driven solutions for real-world problems

## Leadership
The center operates under the expert leadership of Dr. Rodayna Hmede, who serves as the Head of HAICI.

## Team Structure
HAICI is divided into several specialized student and faculty-led teams:

### IT and Technical Team
Responsible for software development, AI model integration, and maintaining the center's digital infrastructure.

### Social Media and Marketing Team (Digital Marketing and Content Strategy)
Dedicated to digital outreach, public relations, and branding strategies. This division is co-led by Mariam Hassan Badawi.

### Research and Development Team
Focuses on exploring new AI trends, developing ethical tech frameworks, and writing academic papers.

### Event and Project Management Team
Organizes workshops, hackathons, and external university representation.

## Key Initiatives and Events

### National Youth Strategy (2026-2030)
HAICI actively represents the IUL Faculty of Engineering in governmental consultations regarding the digital sphere and youth empowerment.

### Designing Hope in Action Hackathon
The center participates in and organizes hackathons focused on designing digital solutions for mental health and community wellbeing.

### Workshops and Seminars
HAICI provides ongoing training in:
- Advanced AI tools
- Cybersecurity integration
- Modern digital creation

## Current Projects
HAICI develops AI-driven projects to serve the IUL community, including:
- The IUL AI Kiosk: A bilingual (Arabic and English) conversational AI assistant for the university campus, capable of answering questions about IUL via voice and text using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology.
- Digital content strategies for IUL's online presence
- Research initiatives in AI ethics and humanitarian applications of AI
