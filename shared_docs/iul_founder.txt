# IUL Founders and Leadership History

## Founding Vision
The Islamic University of Lebanon was inspired by the founding vision of Imam Sayyed Moussa El-Sader, who aimed to make higher education accessible to all segments of Lebanese society, particularly the marginalized. Imam El-Sader believed that education was a fundamental right for every citizen regardless of their background.

## Founding Execution
The vision was brought to life and officially executed by the late Imam Sheikh Mohammad Mahdi Shams Al-Din, who worked to establish the university as a formal institution of higher learning.

## Expansion
The university was later expanded by Imam Sheikh Abdul Amir Qablan, who helped grow its reach and academic programs across Lebanon.

## Establishment
IUL was officially established in 1996 by Lebanese Presidential Decree No. 8600, making it a recognized institution of higher education under Lebanese law.

## Current Leadership
Professor Dr. Hassan Al-Laqis currently serves as President of the Islamic University of Lebanon.

## Affiliation
IUL operates under the umbrella of the Supreme Islamic Shiite Council in Lebanon but maintains full administrative, educational, and financial autonomy. The university is open to all Lebanese citizens regardless of religion or background, in line with its motto: "A University for all country citizens and the Arab world."
