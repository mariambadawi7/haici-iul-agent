# IUL Admissions — How to Apply

## Application Deadlines
- Fall Semester: Deadline is August 30th
- Spring Semester: Deadline is January 15th

## General Admission Process
Prospective students must submit a completed application (form ADM001) to the Admissions and Orientation Office, along with required documents within the academic calendar deadlines. The file and its contents remain the property of the university and its confidentiality is guaranteed. Providing false information or forged documents leads to cancellation of the application.

Important: A student is not officially registered until they pay course fees for the relevant semester. The minimum registered credits cannot be less than one course from those specified in the accepted program.

## Documents Required for Bachelor's (Level 1) Admission
- Certified copy of ID or valid passport (mandatory for foreign students)
- Two recent passport photos certified by the local mayor/municipality
- Certified copy of grades from the last three years of secondary school
- Certified copy of Lebanese Baccalaureate certificate or equivalent, certified by the Ministry of Education and Higher Education
- Social Security affiliation certificate (new and original)
- For transfer students: certified transcript, course descriptions, and a withdrawal letter from the previous university

## Documents Required for Master's (Level 2) Admission
For students coming from outside IUL:
- ID or valid passport copy
- Two recent certified passport photos
- Lebanese Baccalaureate certificate or equivalent, certified by the Ministry of Education
- Equivalency of university degrees, certified by the Ministry of Education (except Lebanese University degrees)
- Official transcript of previous academic record
- Two recommendation letters from academic references who know the student well (sent directly to the Admissions Office in sealed envelopes)
- Personal letter explaining reasons for choosing IUL
- CV or e-portfolio
- Work experience certificates (if applicable)
- Social Security certificate (new and original)

## General Admission Requirements

### Bachelor's Level (Level 1)
- Must hold Lebanese Baccalaureate or equivalent recognized by Lebanon:
  - Lebanese Baccalaureate: Life Sciences (SV), Arts and Humanities (LH), Social and Economic Sciences (SE), Mathematics (SG)
  - Technical Baccalaureate: acceptance depends on alignment between technical field and university program
- Must fill online registration form or visit the Admissions and Orientation Office
- Must demonstrate proficiency in the program's language of instruction (English, French, Arabic, or bilingual)
- Must pay application file fees and entrance exam fees (non-refundable)

### Master's Level (Level 2)
- Must hold a Bachelor's degree or equivalent recognized by the Lebanese Ministry of Education
- Must fulfill specific program admission requirements
- Must pay application file fees

## Conditional/Preliminary Admission
If a student holding a Lebanese Baccalaureate submits an incomplete file, they receive conditional acceptance as a temporarily registered student. They must complete missing documents before the end of the first semester. If not completed by end of the semester, the student cannot renew registration and must sign a withdrawal form (RPRA007) until the file is complete, then can apply for re-registration (RPRA008).

## PhD Admission (Level 3)

### Phase 1 – Required Documents
- Certified copies of all previous university degrees (License and Master's) plus certified transcripts
- Equivalency of degrees before enrolling in PhD
- Certified ID copy or family registration extract (Lebanese students) or valid passport (foreign students)
- Two recent certified passport photos
- Two recommendation letters from academic faculty members who know the student well (sent directly to the Admissions Office in sealed envelopes)
- CV mentioning any published research in peer-reviewed indexed journals
- Note: Students who have published at least two papers in peer-reviewed indexed journals may be exempt from the entrance exam

### Phase 2 – Additional Documents
- A letter of 400-500 words explaining the main goal of pursuing a PhD in a specific field and the scientific and professional benefit of the intended thesis
- A proposed thesis topic as a research project
- A preliminary text of at least 5 pages containing the initial vision for the topic

### Phase 3 – Doctoral Advisory Committee
The university president, upon the dean's recommendation, appoints a Doctoral Advisory Committee from faculty members at the Associate Professor rank or above. The committee sets a written entrance examination (and oral in some specializations) to evaluate the student's ability to pursue doctoral studies.

### PhD Entrance Exam Evaluates:
- Language skills
- General culture level
- Depth of knowledge in the student's specialization
- Research methodology knowledge
- Proficiency in either French or English

### Phase 4 – Post-Acceptance
The faculty provides the PhD candidate with a range of potential thesis topics that have a qualified supervisor available. The supervisor and thesis topic are finalized in consultation with the Doctoral Advisory Committee. A preliminary semester (Pré-Doc) is given for the student to prepare a research plan of 25-30 pages minimum.

## PhD Program Structure (Decree 10068)
Total: 180 European credits (ECTS)
- 30 credits: methodological, research, scientific, and language coursework
- 150 credits: thesis (including 2 original research papers)
- Minimum duration: 3 years; Maximum: 5 years
- A 6th year requires exceptional approval from the university president

## Credit System (ECTS)
IUL adopts the European Credit Transfer System (ECTS).
- 1 credit = 1 hour of instruction per week per semester (15-week semester)
- 1 credit = 10 contact hours per semester
- Bachelor's (3 years): 1,800 hours total
- Bachelor's (4 years): 2,400 hours total
- Bachelor's (5 years): 3,000 hours total
- Master's: 700 hours total, 90 credits for courses + thesis credits
- Academic year = 60 ECTS credits
- Each semester = 14 teaching weeks + 1 final exam week

## Attendance Policy
- Lecture courses: minimum 70% attendance required
- Practical/laboratory courses: minimum 90% attendance required
- Students exceeding absence limit are not allowed to take final exams
- Students must withdraw from the course before the final exam or receive an F grade

## Course Workload
- Full-time Bachelor's students: 24-30 credits per semester
- Maximum 36-38 credits per semester allowed only for final-semester students with GPA ≥ 75/100 (with Dean's approval)
- Full-time Master's students: 30 credits per semester maximum

## Academic Calendar
- Fall Semester: Begins second week of September, ends with finals in late January
- Spring Semester: Begins second week of February, ends with finals in late June
- Summer Session: Optional intensive 6-week term from July to mid-August, for students needing 1-16 credits to complete their degree
