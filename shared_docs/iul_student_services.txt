# IUL Student Services and Campus Life

## Admissions and Orientation Office
The main point of contact for all admission-related processes. Students submit their application files here and can get guidance on program selection, transfer credits, and registration procedures.

## Language Center
The Language Center serves students who need language support in English or French (the main languages of instruction at IUL). It also offers courses in other languages including Italian, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Persian, Turkish, Russian, and more.

The Language Center collaborates with:
- British Council (Lebanon)
- Institut Français (French Embassy in Lebanon)
- Agence Universitaire de la Francophonie (AUF)
- Goethe Institute (German)
- Confucius Institute (Chinese)
- Cervantes Institute (Spanish)
- Istituto Italiano di Cultura (Italian)
- Russian Cultural Center

Students are assessed using the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

## Career Center
The Career Center helps graduates with:
- Career planning and professional guidance
- CV preparation and interview skills
- Annual job fair on campus
- Connecting students with employers through partnership agreements
- Professional training and internship programs
- Networking opportunities

## Student Activities and Clubs
IUL hosts over 30 active student societies covering:
- Sports
- Arts and culture
- Programming and technology
- Debate
- Translation
- Community service
- And more

## Professional Integration
Graduates from IUL faculties are eligible to join Lebanese professional syndicates:
- Engineering and Architecture graduates: eligible for the Order of Engineers and Architects (Beirut/Tripoli)
- Law graduates: eligible for the Bar Associations (Beirut/Tripoli)

## Student Forms and Procedures

### Registration-Related
- RPRA001: Request for maximum credit enrollment (final semester)
- RPRA002: Program change request
- RPRA003: Dual program enrollment request
- RPRA005: Sub-specialization enrollment request
- RPRA006: Conditional admission commitment letter
- RPRA007: Study interruption request
- RPRA008: Re-registration request

### Admission-Related
- ADM001 (AR/EN/FR): University admission application
- ADM002: Transfer application evaluation form
- RPRA008: Readmission form
- RPRA010: Request for admission as free listener/auditor
- RPRA011: Visiting student admission request
- RPRA012.1/12.2: Non-degree student admission
- RPRA013: Foreign visiting student request

### Academic Services
- RPRA009: Petition for additional language remedial session
- RPRA014: Cross-registration request (courses at other universities)
- RPRA016: Request to withdraw from all enrolled courses
- RPRA017: Study interruption form
- RPRA021: Campus change request

### Graduation-Related
- RPRA022: Code of Ethics signing
- RPRA023: Graduation suspension request
- RPRA024: Diploma application
- RPRA025: Authorization for diploma collection by representative
- RPRA026: Duplicate diploma request

## Academic Support

### Center for Continuing Education (CCE)
Offers professional development programs, non-academic certificates, language courses, and specialized corporate training.

### Learning and Teaching Excellence Center (LTEC)
Supports faculty in improving teaching methods and helps graduate students preparing for academic careers.

### Research Center (REC)
Located within each faculty. Promotes research and public service. Coordinated through the Supreme Council for Scientific Research.

### Human Rights Center (HRC)
Promotes human rights culture, tolerance, mutual respect, equal rights, social justice, and civic responsibility. Affiliated with the Faculty of Law.

## Medical and Health Resources
The university provides health-related student support services for students in health-related programs (Physical Therapy, Nursing, Speech Therapy, etc.) who require clinical training and hospital internships.
