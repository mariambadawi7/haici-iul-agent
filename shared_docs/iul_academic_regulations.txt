# IUL Academic Regulations — Grading, Transfer, and Student Rules

## Grading System (ECTS)
IUL uses the European ECTS grading system.

Passing grades:
- General passing grade: 10/20 or 50/100 (ECTS) or 60/100 (American credit system)
- Engineering and some specialized programs: passing grade is 12/20 or 60/100 (ECTS)
- Master's level: minimum passing grade for transfer is 55/100 or 11/20 (ECTS), or 70/100 (American system)

Alphabetic grades (ECTS): A, B, C, D (passing); F (fail)
Note: Faculty of Engineering and Faculty of Public Health do not accept grade D for course transfers.

## Transfer of Credits (Course Transfer Rules)

### General Rules
- A course whose prerequisite has not been validated cannot be transferred. Both the prerequisite and the dependent course must be transferred together.
- The transferred course must have the same contact hours and cover at least 75% of the equivalent course content at IUL.
- Maximum transferable credits: 50% of total program credits (per Ministerial Decree No. 780/3/2013)
- Exception: Engineering Faculty allows up to 120 ECTS transfer out of 300 ECTS total.

### Transfer Codes on Transcript
- "T" = transferred course (from external university)
- "G" = course exemption/dispensation (granted without a grade, does not affect GPA)
- Internal IUL transfers between faculties: marked with an asterisk, keep their original grade (not "T")

### Engineering Faculty Transfer Requirements
- Courses must come from accredited Engineering programs
- Transfer only accepted from institutions recognized by the Order of Engineers
- Candidates must pass an English Proficiency Test (IUL-EPT) with a minimum score of 60/100

### Courses That Cannot Be Transferred
- Courses obtained through internships or thesis projects
- Courses from a student currently on academic probation at the origin university
- Academic probation means the student received a warning due to insufficient results without being expelled

### Transfer Application Process
Submit transfer file to the Admissions and Orientation Office (BAO) at least 4 weeks before registration begins, including:
- Certified copy of transcript
- Course descriptions and syllabi for each course

### If Student Registers for an Already-Transferred Course
The transfer "T" is removed from the transcript and replaced by the new grade obtained at IUL.

### Courses Completed at IUL Cannot Be Retransferred
Courses that a student completed at IUL cannot be taken elsewhere and retransferred back to IUL.

## Re-admission After Academic Interruption

### 3-Year Interruption
Full re-examination of equivalency file. Courses still in the current curriculum with satisfactory grades will be counted.

### 5-Year Interruption
Without legitimate force majeure justification, the student permanently loses the right to resume their academic path. No credits are recognized except:
- Courses with grade A or B (for Humanities, Tourism, Islamic Studies, Law, Economics, Political Sciences)
- Engineering, Technology, Sciences, and Public Health: recognition after 5 years is NOT automatic; handled on a case-by-case basis

### Master's Level
Minimum duration: 2 years; Maximum: 4 years. Unjustified interruption does not grant any right to extension.

## Academic Probation
- Bachelor's students: two semesters on academic probation leads to conditional admission. After the 3rd semester, if the student still doesn't meet the required average, they must change their specialization.
- Master's students: may only re-register for failed courses once. If they fail again, they must change their specialization.
- Students on academic probation may register for failed courses plus a maximum of 2-3 additional courses.

## Changing Specialization or Program
Students can request a change of specialization using form RPRA002FR. A change between two different programs may allow transfer of general education courses and introductory courses. No transfer of credits from Level 2 to Level 1 or vice versa is permitted.

## Course Withdrawal
Students can withdraw from all courses in a semester using form RPRA016EN. The student will receive grade F if they exceed the absence limit and do not withdraw before the final exam.

## Free Listener (Auditor) Status
Students can apply to attend courses as a free listener (non-degree seeking) using form RPRA010FR/EN.

## Cross Registration
Students can register for courses at other universities using form RPRA014EN.

## Changing Campus
Students can request a campus change using form RPRA021EN (Week 13 deadline).

## Graduation

### Conditions for Graduation
- Complete all required credits for the program
- Achieve the required cumulative GPA
- Complete all general education requirements
- Pay all financial obligations

### Graduation Suspension
Students who are not ready to receive their diploma can apply for graduation suspension using form RPRA023EN/FR.

### Diploma Collection
Students collect their diploma using form RPRA024AR/FR after completing all graduation requirements.

### Duplicate Diploma
Students who lose their diploma can request a duplicate using form RPRA026AR.

## Master's Thesis Process

### Key Steps
1. Choose thesis topic and supervisor (form 001)
2. Appoint supervisor (form 002)
3. Calendar for supervision and follow-up (form 003)
4. Pre-defense announcement (form 007)
5. Thesis defense announcement (form 013)
6. Defense evaluation (form 014/015)
7. Thesis submission (form 011)

### Thesis Requirements
- Must publish 2 original research papers in peer-reviewed indexed journals before the thesis defense
- Cannot defend thesis without completing all required coursework
- Plagiarism detection is mandatory (form 019FR)

### Changing Thesis Topic or Supervisor
- Changing thesis topic: use form 009AR
- Changing thesis supervisor: use form 022

### Contestation of Results
Students can contest defense results using form 018FR.

## Code of Ethics
All students must sign the IUL Code of Ethics (form RPRA022EN) upon admission.
