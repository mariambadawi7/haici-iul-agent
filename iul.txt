The **Islamic University of Lebanon (IUL)** is a prominent private, non-profit higher education institution in Lebanon. Established to promote higher education and provide innovative academic programs, IUL has grown into a major teaching-centered research university with a strong presence across the country.

Here is a comprehensive overview of the university:

### **1. History and Foundation**

* **Establishment:** IUL was officially established in 1996 by Lebanese Presidential Decree No. 8600.
* **Founding Vision:** The university was initially inspired by the vision of Imam Sayyed Moussa El-Sader, who aimed to make higher education accessible to all segments of Lebanese society, particularly the marginalized.
* **Execution:** The vision was brought to life by the late Imam Sheikh Mohammad Mahdi Shams Al-Din and was later expanded by Imam Sheikh Abdul Amir Qablan.
* **Affiliation:** It operates under the umbrella of the **Supreme Islamic Shiite Council** in Lebanon but maintains administrative, educational, and financial autonomy. Its motto is "A University for all country citizens and the Arab world."

### **2. Accreditation and Rankings**

* **Local Recognition:** All degrees are fully recognized by the Lebanese Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MEHE).
* **International Accreditation:** IUL holds a prestigious 5-year unreserved institutional accreditation from **HCERES** (the French High Council for Evaluation of Research and Higher Education), marking its compliance with international and European educational standards.
* **Rankings:** IUL regularly appears in regional and global rankings, notably being ranked in the #1001-1200 bracket globally in the QS World University Rankings and placing well within the top 100 in the Arab Region Rankings.

### **3. Campuses**

IUL has strategically expanded to cover major geographic regions in Lebanon, ensuring accessibility for students across the country:

* **Khaldeh Campus (Main Campus):** The headquarters of the university, housing the main administration, the central library, and a wide array of faculties.
* **Wardanieh Campus:** A modern, newly built campus that serves as the primary hub for the Faculty of Engineering and advanced technological studies.
* **Tyre (Sour) Campus:** Established in the 2007-2008 academic year to serve the southern region of Lebanon, alleviating the need for students to commute to Beirut.
* **Baalbek Campus:** Opened in 2014 to bring higher education to the Bekaa Valley and the Baalbek-Hermel region, focusing heavily on applied research, technology, and regional societal needs.

### **4. Faculties and Academic Programs**

IUL offers Undergraduate (Bachelor's), Graduate (Master's), and Postgraduate (PhD) programs across a diverse range of disciplines. The university is home to the following core faculties:

* **Faculty of Engineering:** Located primarily at the Wardanieh campus. IUL was the first university in Lebanon to offer specialized degrees in Biomedical Engineering and Surveying Engineering. It also offers Civil, Computer and Communication, and Food Industry Engineering.
* **Faculty of Law:** A highly regarded faculty offering Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD degrees in Public, Private, and Business Law.
* **Faculty of Economics and Business Administration:** Offers programs in Accounting, Management, Banking and Finance, Information Management, and specialized MBAs (including Hospital Management and Educational Administration).
* **Faculty of Nursing and Health Sciences:** Provides critical healthcare degrees including Nursing, Physical Therapy, Speech Therapy, Midwifery, Nutrition & Dietetics, and Laboratory Sciences.
* **Faculty of Letters and Human Sciences:** Offers programs in Translation, Psychology, Philosophy, History, Geography, and multiple language/literature tracks (Arabic, French, English).
* **Faculty of Political, Administrative, and Diplomatic Sciences:** Offers degrees up to the PhD level in Political Science and International/Diplomatic Relations.
* **Faculty of Tourism Sciences:** Features unique programs in Hotel Management, Travel & Airline Services, Tour Guiding, and Health Tourism.
* **Faculty of Islamic Studies:** Offers comprehensive studies in Islamic theology and jurisprudence (Bachelor's to PhD).

### **5. Student Life and Facilities**

* **Languages:** While Arabic is the native language, many programs (especially Engineering, Business, and Health Sciences) are heavily taught in English and French. The university has a dedicated language center to build these competencies.
* **Extracurriculars:** IUL hosts over 30 active student societies ranging from sports and arts to programming, debate, and translating clubs.
* **Professional Integration:** Graduates from faculties like Engineering and Law are fully eligible to join Lebanese professional syndicates (e.g., the Order of Engineers and Architects in Beirut/Tripoli, and the Beirut/Tripoli Bar Associations).
